01 Live

The Ghritam Cafe — Digital Menu & Direct Ordering Family-run cafe, Mithanpura, Muzaffarpur

I designed, built and deployed the cafe's ordering website: a searchable 105-item menu across 14 categories with dish photos, an on-site cart and WhatsApp checkout — so the cafe keeps the ~33% commission delivery platforms take.

  • Zomato-style add-ons & customisation (extra cheese, toppings, dips) with per-combo cart lines
  • Cart-aware “Recommended for you” strip — suggestions change with what's already in the cart
  • Today's Special cards that deep-link to each dish; franchise enquiry form
  • WhatsApp ordering automation in development: Python state machine, prepaid UPI links, Rapido delivery
